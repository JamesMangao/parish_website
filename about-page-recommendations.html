Sto. Rosario Parish, Pacita, San Pedro, Laguna

Prepared August 18, 2026  ·  Review of public-facing About page content & structure

This document reviews the current About page at /about and provides actionable content recommendations to improve visitor engagement, clarify the parish's identity, and make the page more useful for first-time visitors. The page currently features a Hero section, Stats bar, "Our Calling" narrative, Seven Sacraments, historical Timeline, embedded video, Patron Saints, Leadership, Find Us (with map), and a CTA.

What's Working Well

  • Visual design — The maroon/gold/cream palette, Playfair Display headings, and card-based layouts are polished and cohesive.
  • Content structure — The section flow (Hero → Stats → Narrative → Timeline → Leadership → Map → CTA) is logical and easy to follow.
  • Interactive elements — The horizontal timeline with drag-to-scroll, expand/collapse "Read more", and the Leaflet map are thoughtful touches.
  • Accessibility basics — Reduced-motion respect, aria-labels on buttons, and lazy-loading on the iframe are in place.

1 Replace Sacraments with Mission & Ministries

The Seven Sacraments section describes universal Catholic doctrine — it doesn't communicate anything specific about this parish. Visitors to an About page want to learn about your community, not catechism.

Recommended replacement content:

  • Mission & Vision statement — A concise 2–3 sentence mission and vision for the parish. This is the single most important addition: it gives the page a clear spiritual anchor and tells visitors what Sto. Rosario Parish stands for.
  • Parish Ministries Overview — A card grid showcasing the 6–8 key ministries (e.g., Youth Ministry, Choir, Catechists, Social Services, Liturgical Ministry, Bible Study). Each card can include a short description and a "Learn More" link. This makes the parish feel active and gives visitors a concrete way to get involved.

Suggested Location

Place the Mission & Ministries section immediately after the "Our Calling" narrative and before the Timeline. Move the Sacraments content to a dedicated /sacraments page if desired.

2 Add a Mission & Vision Section

The current "Our Calling" section describes the patroness image and cultural heritage beautifully, but it never explicitly states the parish's mission or values. A clear mission/vision gives the page a focal point and helps visitors understand the parish's purpose.

Suggested structure:

  • A brief Mission statement (1–2 sentences) — What does this parish exist to do?
  • A Vision statement (1–2 sentences) — What is the parish striving to become?
  • Optionally, 3–4 core values as icon cards (e.g., Faith, Community, Service, Heritage)

Content Note

If a formal mission statement already exists in parish documents, this is the place to surface it. If not, consider collaborating with the Parish Pastoral Council to draft one.

3 Expand the Stats Bar

The Stats bar currently shows only Years of Service and Year Founded. This is valuable but underutilized. Adding 1–2 more metrics would make the parish feel vibrant and active.

Recommended additional stats:

  • Active Ministries — Number of ministries or active groups (e.g., "12+")
  • Weekly Masses — Number of masses celebrated each week (e.g., "14")
  • Parishioners Served — Approximate registered families or weekly attendance (use a rounded estimate)
  • Years of Patroness Enthronement — Years since the image was enshrined (calculated from 1983)

Implementation Note

These values can be stored in the existing $global_settings system or computed dynamically. The existing count-up animation already supports additional stat cells.

4 Add an Inline Mass Schedule Summary

The CTA section at the bottom links to the Mass Schedule page, but there's no preview. A compact inline summary reduces clicks and immediately serves visitors who just want to know when Mass is.

Suggested format:

  • A small "Next Mass" or "Regular Schedule" card above the CTA buttons
  • Pull data from the existing schedule settings or hardcoded defaults
  • Include Sunday and weekday times in a compact two-column layout
  • Add a "View full schedule →" link to the dedicated page

5 Extend the Leadership Section

The Leadership section currently shows only the Parish Priest (and optionally an Assistant). To give visitors a fuller picture of parish life, consider adding:

  • Lay leaders — Parish Pastoral Council chairperson, head catechist, choir director, or similar key volunteers
  • Ministry leaders — Brief bios with contact info for major ministries
  • A smaller card format for lay leaders (similar to the existing "Former Parish Priests" grid) to visually distinguish them from clergy

Content Management

Lay leader data could be stored as a JSON array in the existing settings system (similar to how former_priests is managed), with name, role, photo, and optional quote fields.

6 Add a "How to Get Here" Transport Guide

The Find Us section includes a map and address, but first-time visitors often need more context. Consider adding a brief paragraph below the map with:

  • Jeepney / bus routes that pass near the parish
  • Landmark-based directions (e.g., "Across from Pacita Complex, near the main gate")
  • Parking information — Is there on-street parking? A parish parking area?
  • Nearest public transit stops

7 Add Social Proof & Community Highlights

The page is well-structured but could benefit from more warmth and personality. Consider adding:

  • Parishioner testimonial — A short quote from a longtime member about what the parish means to them
  • Photo gallery strip — A small carousel of parish events (fiestas, outreach, youth activities) to show community life
  • A "What to Expect on Your First Visit" blurb — A welcoming 2–3 sentence note about what happens during Mass, where to park, and that visitors are warmly welcomed

8 Technical Notes

  • Leaflet CSS/JS loading — The map library is loaded inline on every About page render. Consider extracting this to a shared layout or conditionally loading it only when the map section is present to reduce page weight.
  • Scroll-reveal attributes — The data-reveal attributes are present throughout the markup but the scroll-reveal JS implementation isn't in the About page file. Confirm it's wired up in a shared script and functioning correctly.
  • Timeline data management — The parish timeline is stored as a raw JSON string in $global_settings['parish_timeline']. Editing raw JSON in a settings field is fragile. Consider building a small admin CRUD for timeline entries to reduce errors.
